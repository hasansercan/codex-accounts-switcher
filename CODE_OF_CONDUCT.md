# Community guidelines

Treat contributors and users with respect. Critique ideas and code, not people. Harassment, threats, discrimination, publishing private information and sharing live credentials are not acceptable.

Keep reports relevant and reproducible. Do not post personal auth files, OAuth links, passwords or 2FA keys. For security issues, use the private report route in SECURITY.md.

The maintainer may edit or remove unsafe content, close off-topic discussions, or restrict participation to protect users. Use GitHub's private abuse-reporting tools for personal safety concerns. These guidelines apply to this repository's issues, pull requests and discussions.
